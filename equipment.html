Tank Size First Always buy the largest tank your space and budget allows. Bigger tanks are more stable and forgiving for beginners.
Filter Turnover Rate Your filter should cycle the tank volume at least 5–10x per hour. Oversizing is always better than undersizing.
Lighting Spectrum Matters For planted tanks, choose full-spectrum LED with WRGB chips. Fish-only tanks can use any good quality LED.
Match Food to Species Herbivores, carnivores, and omnivores all have different nutritional needs. Using species-appropriate food prevents deficiencies.
Heater Wattage Guide Plan 3–5 watts per litre for tropical setups. Consider two smaller heaters for safety redundancy in large tanks.
Test Kit Investment Don't skip a water test kit. Knowing your parameters (pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate) prevents 90% of fish health problems.
