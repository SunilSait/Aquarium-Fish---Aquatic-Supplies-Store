Why Live Plants?

Natural Beauty + Real Benefits

Live aquatic plants aren't just decorative — they actively improve water quality, reduce algae growth, and create a natural environment where fish thrive.

Natural Oxygenation Plants produce oxygen during photosynthesis, naturally enriching tank water.
Nitrate Absorption Living plants consume nitrates, reducing harmful toxins in the water column.
Fish Enrichment Provide natural hiding spots, breeding grounds, and territory for fish.
Algae Control Healthy plants outcompete unwanted algae by consuming the same nutrients.

Guide

Plant Lighting Requirements

Match your plants to the right light level for healthy, thriving growth.

Low Light

Tolerates minimal lighting. Perfect for beginners and low-tech setups without CO2.

  • Anubias species
  • Java Fern
  • Java Moss
  • Cryptocoryne
  • Vallisneria

Medium Light

Requires moderate lighting. Some species benefit from CO2 supplementation for best growth.

  • Amazon Sword
  • Rotala species
  • Monte Carlo
  • Ludwigia
  • Hygrophila

High Light

Demands strong lighting and often pressurized CO2. Rewarding for expert aquascapers.

  • Hemianthus (HC)
  • Dwarf Hairgrass
  • Glossostigma
  • Eleocharis
  • Riccia fluitans

Setting Up Your Planted Tank

Follow our simple guide to create a thriving planted aquascape from day one.

Choose Your Substrate

Nutrient-rich aqua soil or gravel provides the foundation for strong root development.

Plan Your Layout

Apply the rule of thirds. Foreground short, midground varied, background tall.

Plant & Cycle

Plant densely from the start to outcompete algae. Cycle your tank for 2–4 weeks.

Maintain & Trim

Regular trimming and fertilization keep your scape looking pristine and growing healthy.
