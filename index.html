Essential Care Tips

From water chemistry to feeding habits — our experts share the knowledge that keeps your fish thriving.

01
Water Quality

Master Your Water Chemistry

Water parameters are the foundation of a healthy aquarium. Get these right and your fish will flourish naturally.

  • Test pH, ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate weekly
  • Perform 20–30% water changes bi-weekly
  • Use a quality dechlorinator every change
  • Monitor temperature with a reliable thermometer
Read Full Guide →
02
Nutrition

Balanced Feeding Habits

Proper nutrition enhances color, boosts immunity, and promotes natural behavior. Less is often more when it comes to feeding.

  • Feed only what fish can consume in 2–3 minutes
  • Vary diet between flakes, pellets, and live food
  • Fast your fish one day per week for gut health
  • Match food type to species-specific needs
